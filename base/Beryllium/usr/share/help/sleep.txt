Usage: sleep NUMBER[SUFFIX]...
  or:  sleep OPTION

Pause for NUMBER seconds. SUFFIX may be s, m, h, or d (seconds
implied). Multiple NUMBER[SUFFIX] operands are summed together.

  -h   display this help and exit

Examples:
  sleep 5          pause for 5 seconds
  sleep 1m 30s     pause for 1 minute 30 seconds
